How does the heroine from Saekano continue to inspire fans worldwide?

Megumi Kato is the main heroine of Saekano: How to Raise a Boring Girlfriend. She is a talented, beautiful, and humble girl who serves as the inspiration for the game the main characters create. Despite her calm and reserved nature, Megumi has a deep passion for creativity and a kind heart.
